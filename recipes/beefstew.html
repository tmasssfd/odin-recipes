Ingredients

  • 1 lb Boneless Beef Chuck
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 large potato
  • 1 large pepper
  • 1 stalk of celery
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 Bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp flour/li>
  • 3 cups water + water for roux

Directions

  1. Sautee beef until it is browned
  2. Add 3 cups of water
  3. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer covered for 2 hours.
  4. Stir in remaining ingredients except for flour
  5. Mix 1/4 cup water and 2 tbsp flour in separate container. Add to stew and stir
  6. Heat until desired consistency
