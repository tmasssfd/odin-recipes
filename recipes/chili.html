Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp cocoa
  • 16 ounce can diced tomatoes
  • 16 ounce can kidney beans
  • 16 ounce can black beans

Directions

  1. Cook ground beef until browned
  2. Add garlic and cook for 2 minutes
  3. Add all other ingredients
  4. Heat until boiling
  5. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes
