Ingredients

  • 1 lb Ground Beef
  • 1/2 cup dry bread crumbs
  • 1/4 cup of milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp worcestershire sauce
  • 1/4 tsp pepper
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 egg

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 F
  2. Mix together all ingredients except for ground beef in a bowl
  3. mix in ground beef
  4. form mixture into balls and place on a baking sheet
  5. Cook for 15 minutes
